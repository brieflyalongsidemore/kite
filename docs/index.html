How it decides

The writer proposes. Jev decides.

Most AI writing tools hand you whatever the model says first. Kite makes the writer come up with options, then asks Jev to judge them with real probabilities. You see every call and why it went that way.

Example plan for an AI-tools account

From profile to posted

Four steps, and it remembers each one.

  1. 1 · Learn

    It learns your voice

    Paste your profile page, drop in screenshots or upload an export like X's tweets.js. No paid API needed. You get an editable profile of your niche, audience and tone.

  2. 2 · Plan

    It makes a plan

    Research from Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, Mastodon and Google Trends, then a mix of posts, ideas, drafts and growth moves, each one judged by Jev.

  3. 3 · Work

    It helps you do it

    Every action has Work on it, which opens a thread with a checklist and drafts. Jev picks the best ones. You post them yourself.

  4. 4 · Remember

    It keeps a brain

    What you did, skipped and learned goes into a folder of linked Markdown notes. Reflect updates where you're really heading.

      Bring your own models

      Any writer. Jev as the judge.

      Pick a writer in Settings or in .env. Each one has a backup model: if the main one stays overloaded, Kite waits 15, 30 and 60 seconds, then carries on with the backup from where it stopped.
